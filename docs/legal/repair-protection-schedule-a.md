# SCHEDULE A — WORKMANSHIP WARRANTY AND REPAIR PROTECTION UNDERTAKING

**Version:** v1.0
**Effective from:** date of electronic acceptance
**Parties:** FixBudi (the "Platform") and the accepting repair business (the "Repair Partner")

This Schedule A forms an integral part of the FixBudi Repair Partner Agreement. Acceptance is a condition of receiving mobile phone and laptop repair jobs through the Platform. Where this Schedule conflicts with the main agreement, this Schedule prevails on the subject matter of workmanship warranty.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Covered Repair"** means any repair of a mobile phone or laptop performed by the Repair Partner on a job originating from the Platform.

1.2 **"Same Fault"** means the recurrence of the specific defect, symptom or malfunction that the Covered Repair was engaged to remedy, including a defect in any part fitted during that repair.

1.3 **"Warranty Period"** means ninety (90) calendar days beginning on the date the device is returned to, or collected by, the Customer following the Covered Repair.

1.4 **"Repair Protection Plan"** means the optional paid plan offered by the Platform to a Customer which funds the logistics and handling of exercising the warranty in this Schedule. It is a service guarantee arrangement and **not** a contract of insurance.

1.5 **"Customer"** means the end user who engaged the Covered Repair through the Platform.

---

## 2. THE UNDERTAKING

2.1 The Repair Partner warrants that every Covered Repair will be carried out with reasonable care and skill, using parts of merchantable quality fit for their purpose, in accordance with section 130 and section 131 of the **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act, 2018 (FCCPA)**.

2.2 The Repair Partner undertakes that if the Same Fault occurs within the Warranty Period, it shall re-diagnose and re-repair the device, or replace the defective part fitted, **at no charge whatsoever** to the Customer or to the Platform, whether for labour, parts, testing or handling.

2.3 The Repair Partner shall commence work on a device returned under clause 2.2 within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt and shall complete the re-repair within seventy-two (72) hours of receipt, save where a part must be sourced, in which case it shall notify the Platform in writing of a revised completion date.

2.4 Where the Same Fault cannot be remedied on the second attempt, the Repair Partner shall, at the Platform's election, either (a) refund the full repair fee received for the Covered Repair, or (b) bear the documented cost of remedying the fault through an alternative repair partner nominated by the Platform.

2.5 The Repair Partner shall not require, request or accept any payment from the Customer in respect of a re-repair falling within clause 2.2. Any such payment collected shall be refunded immediately and shall constitute a material breach of this Schedule.

---

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The undertaking in clause 2 does not extend to:

(a) a new fault or a fault unrelated to the Covered Repair;

(b) liquid ingress, impact, crushing, burning or electrical surge damage occurring after the device left the Repair Partner's custody;

(c) opening, servicing, modification or attempted repair of the device by any person other than the Repair Partner or a partner nominated by the Platform, where evidence of such interference is documented;

(d) software or firmware changes, rooting, jailbreaking, unauthorised operating system installation or malware introduced by the Customer;

(e) consumable items, including batteries beyond a stated capacity tolerance of eighty per cent (80%) of rated capacity, save where the battery was itself the subject of the Covered Repair;

(f) cosmetic wear not affecting function;

(g) loss, theft or total destruction of the device.

3.2 A Repair Partner asserting an exclusion bears the burden of substantiating it with photographic or diagnostic evidence submitted through the Platform within forty-eight (48) hours of receiving the claim.

---

## 4. EVIDENCE AND RECORD KEEPING

4.1 The Repair Partner shall, for every Covered Repair, record in the Platform: the fault diagnosed, the work performed, the parts fitted with identifiers where available, and dated condition photographs of the device at intake and at release.

4.2 Failure to maintain the records in clause 4.1 raises a rebuttable presumption in favour of the Customer in any dispute under this Schedule.

4.3 Records shall be retained for not less than twenty-four (24) months and shall be produced to the Platform or to a competent regulator on request.

---

## 5. CLAIMS, MEDIATION AND DISPUTES

5.1 A claim is initiated by the Customer through the Platform. The Platform notifies the Repair Partner, which shall accept or contest the claim within forty-eight (48) hours. Silence beyond that period is deemed acceptance.

5.2 A contested claim is referred to Platform mediation. The Platform shall consider the intake and release records, the Customer's evidence, and any diagnostic report, and shall issue a written determination within seven (7) days.

5.3 Where a claim is upheld, the Repair Partner bears the cost of the re-repair and the Platform bears the pickup and return logistics from the Repair Protection Plan fund. Where a claim is not upheld, the Platform may recover from the Customer any logistics cost not covered by a Plan.

5.4 A party dissatisfied with a determination under clause 5.2 may refer the matter to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the **Arbitration and Mediation Act, 2023**. The seat of arbitration shall be the capital city of the State in which the Repair Partner's principal place of business is situate, and the proceedings shall be in the English language. For Repair Partners in Rivers State, the seat shall be Port Harcourt.

5.5 Nothing in this Schedule limits the Customer's statutory rights under the FCCPA, the Sale of Goods Act as applicable in the relevant State, or any applicable State consumer protection law, nor the right of a Customer to complain to the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission or to the relevant State consumer protection agency.

---

## 6. CONSEQUENCES OF BREACH

6.1 Where the Repair Partner refuses or fails to honour clause 2, the Platform may, without prejudice to any other remedy:

(a) charge back to the Repair Partner the repair fee received for the Covered Repair;

(b) recover from the Repair Partner the cost of remedying the fault elsewhere;

(c) withhold payouts to the extent of amounts in (a) and (b);

(d) suspend the Repair Partner from receiving new jobs; and

(e) after two (2) upheld claims of refusal within any six (6) month period, delist the Repair Partner from the Platform.

6.2 Set-off under clause 6.1(c) shall be notified in writing with a statement of account.

---

## 7. REPAIR PROTECTION PLAN — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

7.1 The Repair Protection Plan is a contract between the Platform and the Customer. The Repair Partner is not a party to it, receives no part of the plan fee, and acquires no liability under it beyond the undertaking in clause 2.

7.2 The Platform does not hold itself out as an insurer, does not underwrite risk of loss or damage to the device, and does not carry on insurance business within the meaning of the **Insurance Act**. The plan fee purchases logistics, handling and administration of the warranty in this Schedule.

7.3 The undertaking in clause 2 applies to every Covered Repair whether or not the Customer purchased a Repair Protection Plan. The plan affects only who bears transport and handling costs.

---

## 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 Each party shall process Customer personal data received under this Schedule only for the purposes of performing the repair and administering claims, and shall comply with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023**.

8.2 The Repair Partner shall not retain Customer data extracted from a device, shall not access Customer content beyond what is strictly necessary for diagnosis and testing, and shall report any personal data breach to the Platform within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.

---

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 This Schedule is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and, in respect of matters within State legislative competence, by the laws of the State in which the Repair Partner operates.

9.2 The Platform may amend this Schedule on thirty (30) days' written notice. Continuing to accept jobs after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the amended version. Claims are governed by the version in force at the date of the Covered Repair.

9.3 Electronic acceptance of this Schedule through the Platform, evidenced by the acceptor's name, user account, timestamp and network address, is valid and binding under the **Evidence Act, 2011 (as amended)** and constitutes the Repair Partner's signature.

9.4 Nothing in this Schedule creates a partnership, agency, joint venture or employment relationship between the parties.

---

By checking the acceptance box and entering their full name in the Platform, the person accepting confirms that they are duly authorised to bind the Repair Partner and that the Repair Partner accepts and will be bound by this Schedule A, version v1.0.
